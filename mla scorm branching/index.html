The Rights of Dogs

While most discussion surrounding animal rights is focused on our consumption of meat and the treatment of farm animals, there is something missing from the general discussion. It's important to note, "We may feel comfortable with our dogs, but that doesn't mean they're living life to the fullest."

We forget that our best friends, domesticated dogs, often do not receive the necessary kindness for full comfort in the home. In the book, K9 Revolution, it's noted that dogs who are crated for over eight hours a day often have a behavioral response that shows discontent or discomfort (Thomas 19). This is often shown through a dog's anxieties and hyperactivity. At the same time, dogs tied to a line outside can show a unique form of anxiety, including panic in open spaces, or excessive excitement when approached by a family member (Thomas 12). The Home Dog Breeders Association points out that the best way to provide comfort for your dog is to allow them as much free movement as possible while owners are away ("Questions of Treatment").
